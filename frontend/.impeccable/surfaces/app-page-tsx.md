## Direction contract

THESIS: Hookclip turns a night of GTA VI streams into money, and the landing page proves it in the first viewport by showing the product doing its job: a ranked moment list with scores, hooks, and a 9:16 clip. It refuses the generic SaaS hero (eyebrow label, headline, metric strip, card grid) and the category's dark-mode costume (purple-black, neon glow, gradients, glass). The mechanism is the visual.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#0a0a0b), hairline borders (#232326), white display type with tight tracking, gray secondary text that holds 4.5:1, one red accent (#e63b2e) used only for actions and the top score. System font stack — no webfont, no display face. No gradients, no glow, no glass, no bento, no cards as page structure. Hairline-divided rows, precise numerals, real product UI as the only visual. Dark is earned by the scene: clippers work at night, in dark rooms, watching streams — the tool lives where the footage lives.

STORY: The visitor is a clipper on GTA VI launch week, deciding whether a $9 tool is worth it. In one viewport they understand Hookclip finds the moments worth clipping and that brands pay per 1,000 views; they believe it because the ranked list is shown, not claimed; they act by starting free (dashboard) or picking a plan (checkout). Every number on the page is verified (median ~$1 per 1k views, ~$200 per-clip cap) and every earning claim carries the never-guaranteed caveat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky dark header (drawn play-mark logo left, nav, Start clipping button). Centered headline, two lines: "GTA VI launches Nov 19. Clippers get paid." Lede in plain words with the verified median rate. CTA row: Start clipping free (dashboard), See pricing (#pricing). Below, the product panel: ranked moments (score chips, time ranges, reasons, hooks) beside a 9:16 clip frame with captions, captioned "Sample VOD". No eyebrow, no metric strip, no decorative art.

FORM: Dark Linear-style product-led landing. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
