## Direction contract (v2 — Nov 15, 2026. Salal rejected v1 as "totally the same, nothing changes": the dark recolor kept the same section order. Rebuilt from his saved web-design reels.)

THESIS: The page follows @ayzz.thedesigner's landing-page structure exactly — hero (result + fear) → proof → problem → solution → how it works → FAQ → final CTA → footer. The product is not shown until the visitor has seen the proof; features do not come before the problem. The proof is the real product working: a ranked moment list from a real VOD, with the clip frame. No fake testimonials — Hookclip has zero users; the proof is the live demo plus verified earn rates (~$1 per 1k views, ~$200 per-clip cap, never guaranteed).

OWN-WORLD: Same dark world as v1 (near-black #0a0a0b, hairlines #232326, one red #d92d20, system stack, no gradients/glow/glass/bento/cards). What changes is structure and motion, not palette. Motion follows Emil Kowalski's skills (github.com/emilkowalski/skills, from the @strategyfox reel): transform + opacity only, strong ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), no scale(0), one entrance per container, uniform stagger forbidden (shrinking delays), reduced motion collapses the choreography. The motion budget lives in two places only: the hero entrance (title lead, lede + CTAs support, pill fades) and the proof panel's ranked rows staggering in when scrolled into view, plus a slow Ken Burns on the clip frame. Everything else is static.

STORY: The visitor is a clipper on GTA VI launch week, deciding whether a $9 tool is worth it. In the first viewport they see the result they want (clips that pay) and the fear (the wave passing); then the proof (a real VOD turned into ranked clips); then the problem priced in hours (8–10h stream, 30–60s that matter, 7-day earning window); then the solution (6 features, each a benefit + a small visual of the mechanism); then 3 steps, pricing, FAQ, and a benefit-driven final CTA. Every number is verified and every earning claim carries the never-guaranteed caveat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky dark header (drawn play-mark logo left, nav: Proof / Problem / How it works / Pricing / FAQ, Start clipping button). Text-led hero, no product panel: pill "GTA VI launches Nov 19, 2026 · PS5 + Xbox" (fades only), headline "Turn GTA VI streams into clips that pay.", lede, CTA row (Start clipping free → dashboard, See the proof → #proof), fear line "Wait a week and the same clip is worth nothing. The wave does not come back."

FORM: Dark, motion-led, ayzz-structured landing. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
